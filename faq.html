Questions, Answered Honestly

Everything business owners usually ask us before starting. Something missing? Message us — we reply within a day.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most business websites are designed, built and live within days to a couple of weeks, depending on size and how quickly we receive your content (logo, photos, text). We'll give you a clear timeline before we start — and we stick to it.

How much does a website cost?

Every website is different, so we quote per project after a short chat about what you need. Message us on WhatsApp or call us and we'll give you a clear, honest quote — free, with no pressure to proceed.

What do you need from me to get started?

Very little: your logo (if you have one), photos of your business or products, and the basic facts — services, prices, opening hours. Don't have some of it? We'll guide you, and we can work with what you have.

I already have a website. Can you redesign it?

Yes — redesigns are one of our core services. We review your current site, keep what works, and rebuild the rest into a modern, fast, mobile-friendly website with zero downtime during the switch.

Will my website work on phones?

Absolutely. Most of your customers will visit on a phone, so every website we build is mobile-first: fast, readable and easy to use on any screen size, then perfected for tablets and desktops.

Can you update the website for me later?

Yes. With a Care & Maintenance plan we handle updates, backups, security checks and content changes for you — new photos, prices, pages — so your website always stays current without you lifting a finger.

Will customers find my website on Google?

We build every website with the technical foundations Google looks for — fast loading, clean structure, proper titles and descriptions — so your site is ready to be found from day one.

How do we get started?

Just message us on WhatsApp or give us a call. Tell us about your business in a few lines, we'll ask the right questions, and you'll get a free quote and timeline. No forms, no jargon, no obligation.

Ask us directly — a real conversation beats a FAQ page every time.
