The Story Behind the Websites

Every website starts with a business problem. Here's the challenge each client brought us, what we built, and what changed.

Restaurant & Party Palace

Peace Night

A much-loved restaurant and party palace whose customers had no way to explore the menu, see the venue or book a celebration online. Their online presence didn't match the experience they deliver in person.

Visit Live Site
Visit Website ↗

The Challenge

No online menu, no party booking path, and no gallery to show off the venue — customers had to phone or visit just to learn the basics.

What We Built

A rich business website with a full digital menu, a dedicated party palace section, photo galleries, customer reviews and one-tap calling and directions throughout.

The Outcome

The business now has a complete online home: guests browse the menu before they visit, and event planners can explore and enquire about the party palace any time of day.

Makeup Artist

Makeup by Riya

A talented makeup artist relying entirely on social media DMs for bookings — her work was beautiful, but her booking process lost clients in the scroll.

Visit Live Site
Visit Website ↗

The Challenge

Services, prices and portfolio were scattered across social posts. Potential brides couldn't quickly see packages or how to book — and many simply moved on.

What We Built

An elegant portfolio website with dedicated service pages, clear pricing, a gallery of her best work, client reviews and a simple booking enquiry path.

The Outcome

One link now answers every question a client has — what she offers, what it costs and how to book — turning profile visitors into real appointments.

Driving Institute

Alam Driving Institute

A driving institute with a strong local reputation but no website — new students had no way to check courses, prices or timings before calling.

Visit Live Site
Visit Website ↗

The Challenge

Every enquiry started as a phone call asking the same questions — course options, fees, opening hours — and students comparing institutes found competitors first.

What We Built

A clear, trust-building website listing every course with transparent pricing, licence guidelines, real student reviews, opening hours and one-tap call and WhatsApp buttons.

The Outcome

Students now arrive informed and ready to enrol, and the institute presents itself online with the same professionalism it teaches with.

Event Decoration

Himalayan Flower Decoration

A flower decoration business whose stunning wedding and event work lived only in phone galleries and word of mouth — invisible to anyone planning a celebration.

Visit Live Site
Visit Website ↗

The Challenge

Decoration is bought with the eyes. Without a showcase, the business couldn't prove its quality to couples and planners comparing vendors online.

What We Built

A visual-first portfolio website presenting their wedding, stage, car and event decoration work in large, beautiful galleries with an easy enquiry path.

The Outcome

Their craft is now on permanent display — a link they can send any prospect that instantly shows the quality words can't describe.
